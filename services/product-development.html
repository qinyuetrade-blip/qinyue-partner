Product Development

Turn your product idea, drawing, sample or reference product into a manufacturable solution.

What We Do

Qinyue helps international buyers move from concept to a manufacturable product. We support evaluation of ideas, development of specifications, material selection, design adjustments, prototype coordination and matching with suitable Chinese manufacturing resources.

Capabilities

  • Concept evaluation
  • Specification development
  • Material selection
  • Design adjustment support
  • Prototype coordination
  • Sample development
  • Process evaluation
  • Supplier matching

Typical Process

  1. Share your idea or reference — drawings, samples, photos, market requirements
  2. Evaluate feasibility — materials, processes, cost drivers, manufacturing approach
  3. Develop specifications — refine requirements for production
  4. Coordinate prototypes / samples
  5. Match manufacturing resources and prepare for production

Who This Is For

Product brands, startups developing physical products, companies with an idea or reference product who need a path to manufacturing in China.
