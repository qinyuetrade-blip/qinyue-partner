OEM Manufacturing

Your Design → Our Manufacturing Network. Manufacture according to your drawings, specifications, samples and brand requirements.

What OEM Means Here

You already have a complete design, drawing, specification or approved sample. Qinyue helps identify suitable Chinese manufacturers and coordinates production according to your requirements. We do not claim to manufacture everything ourselves — we coordinate production with suitable manufacturing resources.

Suitable For

  • Custom components
  • Industrial products
  • Sports products
  • Consumer products
  • Commercial products
  • Electronic products

OEM Process

  1. Requirements — drawings, specifications, samples, branding, quantity, quality standards
  2. Supplier Matching — identify suitable manufacturers based on process, capacity and experience
  3. Sampling — coordinate prototypes or pre-production samples for approval
  4. Production — coordinate manufacturing according to approved requirements
  5. QC — support quality checks before shipment
  6. Shipment — packaging and shipment preparation

Key Point

Your Design → Our Manufacturing Network

You retain design ownership. We help match and coordinate the manufacturing side.

Can you manufacture according to drawings?
Yes. For suitable projects, Qinyue can coordinate production with Chinese manufacturers based on drawings, specifications and samples.
Do you provide OEM services?
Yes. Qinyue supports OEM projects based on customer designs, specifications, samples and branding requirements.
