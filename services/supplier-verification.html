Supplier Verification

Help evaluate whether a potential Chinese supplier is suitable for your product and project requirements.

What We Do

Qinyue can help evaluate potential Chinese suppliers based on available information and your specific project requirements. We do not claim “100% verified suppliers.” Evaluation is always based on the information available and the needs of the project.

Review Areas

  • Product capability
  • Manufacturing capability
  • Equipment
  • Production experience
  • Certifications (where available and relevant)
  • Samples
  • Communication
  • Commercial information

Typical Steps

  1. Share the supplier details and your product / project requirements
  2. Review available company and capability information
  3. Assess fit against your technical and commercial needs
  4. Recommend next steps (samples, further checks, or alternative options)

Important Note

Supplier evaluation is based on available information and project requirements. For higher-risk or high-value projects, additional third-party audits or on-site checks may be recommended.
