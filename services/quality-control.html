Quality Control

Support product quality checks and production inspection before shipment according to project requirements.

What We Do

Qinyue can coordinate quality checks according to the requirements of each project. We do not claim laboratory certification or independent testing capability unless specific evidence is available for a given project.

Possible Inspection Points

  • Specifications
  • Dimensions
  • Materials
  • Appearance
  • Function
  • Packaging
  • Quantity
  • Production requirements

How It Works

  1. Define quality criteria based on your drawings, samples or specifications
  2. Agree inspection points and acceptance standards
  3. Coordinate checks during or after production
  4. Report findings and support corrective actions if needed
  5. Support pre-shipment readiness

Important Note

Quality coordination is project-based. For regulated products or critical applications, third-party inspection or laboratory testing can be arranged when required.
