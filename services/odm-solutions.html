ODM Solutions

Your Idea → Product Development → Manufacturing. Start with an existing product, concept or market requirement and develop a customized product solution for your brand.

What ODM Means Here

You may have a product idea, an existing reference product or a market requirement, but not a complete manufacturing-ready design. Qinyue helps evaluate options, coordinate customization and connect the project with suitable Chinese manufacturing resources.

Capabilities

  • Existing product customization
  • Functional changes
  • Material changes
  • Appearance changes
  • Packaging and branding
  • Private label support
  • Production coordination

ODM Process

  1. Idea / Reference — share concept, reference product or market need
  2. Product Evaluation — assess suitable base products or platforms
  3. Customization — define changes to function, material, appearance, packaging
  4. Sample — coordinate samples for approval
  5. Production — coordinate manufacturing
  6. QC — support quality checks before shipment

Key Point

Your Idea → Product Development → Manufacturing

ODM is often faster and lower-risk when you need speed to market or want to validate demand before full custom tooling.

Do you provide ODM services?
Yes. Qinyue can support suitable ODM projects involving existing products, concepts or reference products.
