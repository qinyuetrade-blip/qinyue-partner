How It Works

A straightforward process designed to make China sourcing and manufacturing easier.

01

Tell Us What You Need

Send: Product name, Photo, Drawing, CAD, Specification, Product link, Sample, or even a basic Idea. The more detail you provide, the faster we can evaluate suitable options.

02

Source & Evaluate

Qinyue identifies suitable products, suppliers and manufacturing approaches based on your requirements, target quantity, quality expectations and timeline.

03

Sample & Develop

Review samples, specifications, materials and customization requirements. Iterate as needed until the product meets your standards.

04

Produce & Inspect

Coordinate production and quality checks according to project requirements. Key dimensions, materials, appearance and packaging are reviewed before shipment.

05

Ship

Prepare products and coordinate shipment arrangements. Packaging and documentation are handled according to the agreed terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I send?
A product name, photo, drawing, specification, product link, sample or even a basic product idea can be a useful starting point.
Do you only handle custom products?
No. We can also help source existing / ready-made / standard batch products from suitable Chinese suppliers. Custom, OEM and ODM projects are fully supported as well.
Can you do samples and small batches?
Yes. We support samples and small-batch production. For most products that do not require new tooling/molds, sample lead time is typically about 3–7 days. Minimum order quantities (MOQ) depend on the specific product and manufacturing process.
Do you provide OEM services?
Yes. Qinyue supports OEM projects based on customer designs, specifications, samples and branding requirements. Your Design → Our Manufacturing Network.
Do you provide ODM services?
Yes. Qinyue can support suitable ODM projects involving existing products, concepts or reference products. Your Idea → Product Development → Manufacturing.
