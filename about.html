About Qinyue Global

China Sourcing, OEM & ODM Partner

Qinyue Global is a China-based sourcing and OEM partner helping international buyers source, develop and manufacture products in China.

Based in Shenzhen, we connect customers with suitable manufacturing resources across China, supporting projects from product sourcing and development to production coordination and quality control.

Our product categories cover Industrial & Mechanical, Sports & Outdoor, Consumables & Commercial Supplies, Consumer & Lifestyle, and Electronics & Smart Products.

Whether you have a technical drawing, product sample, reference product or simply an idea, we help turn your requirements into practical manufacturing solutions.

Our role is simple: understand what you need, identify the right manufacturing solution, and help you move from product idea to finished product.
