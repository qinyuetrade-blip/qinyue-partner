How Does OEM Manufacturing in China Work?

From requirements and supplier matching to sampling, production, QC and shipment — a clear view of the OEM process for international buyers.

OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) in this context means: you provide the design, drawings, specifications or approved sample; manufacturing is carried out according to your requirements through suitable Chinese manufacturers. Qinyue Global’s role is to match and coordinate — Your Design → Our Manufacturing Network.

Step 1: Requirements

Provide as much clarity as possible:

  • 2D drawings and/or 3D models
  • Material grades and finishes
  • Tolerances and critical dimensions
  • Target quantities and timeline
  • Branding and packaging needs
  • Any certification or inspection requirements

Step 2: Supplier Matching

Based on process, material, volume and quality needs, suitable manufacturers are identified and shortlisted. Not every factory is right for every part — matching capability matters more than the lowest quoted price alone.

Step 3: Sampling

Prototypes or pre-production samples are coordinated for your review. Approve a golden sample in writing before volume production. Sample lead times for many parts without new tooling often fall in the range of several days to a few weeks, depending on complexity.

Step 4: Production

Once samples are approved, production is coordinated according to the agreed specifications, quantity and schedule. Communication on progress and any process questions is handled through the project contact.

Step 5: Quality Control

Inspection points can include dimensions, materials, appearance, function, packaging and quantity — according to project requirements. Pre-shipment checks help reduce the risk of receiving non-conforming goods.

Step 6: Shipment Preparation

Packaging and documentation are prepared for the agreed shipping method. Qinyue Global can support coordination of these steps as part of the overall project.

OEM vs ODM — Quick Reminder

OEM: You own the design; manufacturing follows your specification.
ODM: Starts from an existing product or platform; you brand and may customize within limits.

FAQ

Do I need to own tooling for OEM?
For many machined or assembled products, no new tooling is required. For molded or highly customized products, tooling may be needed — ownership and cost should be clarified in the agreement.
Can Qinyue Global handle small batches?
Yes. Samples and small-batch production are supported. MOQ depends on the product and process.
