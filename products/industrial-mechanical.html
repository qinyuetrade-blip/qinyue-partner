Industrial & Mechanical Products

Engineered components, precision parts and custom manufacturing solutions sourced and produced through qualified Chinese manufacturers.

Product Overview

Qinyue Global helps international buyers source industrial and mechanical products in China — from standard components to fully custom-machined parts. We coordinate supplier selection, sampling, production and quality checks according to your drawings, specifications and volume requirements.

Applications

Machinery, automotive components, industrial equipment, mechanical systems, automation, structural assemblies and replacement parts.

Customization Options

Material, dimensions, tolerance, surface finish, quantity, packaging and branding can be adapted to your project requirements.

OEM / ODM Capability

OEM: Your Design → Our Manufacturing Network. Provide drawings or samples and Qinyue Global coordinates production with suitable Chinese manufacturers.

ODM: Start from a reference product or concept; we help evaluate and develop a suitable manufacturing solution.

Manufacturing Process

  1. Requirements review (drawings, specs, samples)
  2. Supplier matching and process selection
  3. Sampling and approval
  4. Production coordination
  5. Quality checks and shipment preparation

Quality Control

Key dimensions, materials and surface requirements can be checked according to project needs before shipment.

Typical Project Requirements

2D drawings and/or 3D models (STEP/IGES), material grade, tolerances, surface finish, target quantity, packaging and any certification needs.

Can Qinyue Global manufacture according to my drawings?
Yes. For suitable projects, Qinyue Global coordinates production with Chinese manufacturers based on your drawings, specifications and samples.
What information should I send?
Product name, drawings, photos, specifications, material requirements, quantity and any reference links or samples.

Have a Drawing, Sample or Specification?

Send your requirements. Qinyue Global will help identify suitable Chinese manufacturers.
